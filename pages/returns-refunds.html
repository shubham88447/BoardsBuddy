Returns & Refunds Policy

Current Policy for Academic Year 2025–2026. Last updated: October 2026.

100% Free Educational Platform — No Fees Collected

1. Current Free Status

BoardsBuddy is dedicated to supporting Maharashtra State Board Class 10 (SSC) students. All educational resources on BoardsBuddy are provided completely free of charge for the current academic year (2025–2026).

We do not collect any subscription fees, registration charges, payment card information, or one-time micro-payments. Because no financial payments are accepted, returns, cancellations, and monetary refunds are not applicable at this time.

2. Account Registration vs. Paid Subscriptions

Certain advanced sections of our website — specifically Notes, Short Notes, Chapter-wise PYQs, and PYQ Solutions — require users to create an account and sign in. Creating a student account is entirely free and does not involve entering any billing or payment details.

3. Future Policy Provisions

Should BoardsBuddy introduce optional premium features, hard-copy book shipments, or paid test-evaluation services in future academic cycles, an explicit, fair, and legally compliant returns and cancellation policy will be published well before any payment processing is implemented. Users will always receive advance notice of any policy changes.

4. Inquiries & Content Assistance

If you have questions regarding our educational access policy, please contact us via our Contact Us page or open an issue on our official GitHub Repository.
