About BoardsBuddy

A student-first educational platform engineered specifically for Maharashtra State Board Class 10 (SSC) learners.

Our Purpose & Mission

BoardsBuddy was created to eliminate the friction that Maharashtra State Board Class 10 students face while preparing for their board examinations. High-quality study materials, past year papers, chapter-wise questions, and reference textbooks are often scattered across disparate websites, unorganized forum drives, or gated behind expensive paywalls.

Our mission is to bring structure, speed, and focus to your revision through a unified, fast, and modern digital study hub designed strictly around the official Maharashtra Board curriculum.

What BoardsBuddy Offers

📚 Official Textbooks

Direct access to Balbharati e-textbooks for all core subjects, complete with an in-browser PDF reader and offline download options.

📝 Comprehensive Notes

In-depth chapter notes highlighting core conceptual theory, definitions, derivations, experiments, and exercise answers.

⚡ Rapid Short Notes

One-page revision sheets, formula cheat-sheets, key laws, and memory maps curated for last-minute exam review.

🎯 Previous Year Papers (PYQs)

Authentic Maharashtra State Board annual examination question papers from 2019 to 2024 to simulate real test timings.

🔍 Chapter-wise PYQs

Topic-targeted question banks showing historical exam trends, mark weights, and frequently repeated question patterns.

💡 Step-by-Step Solutions

Exemplary model answers and marking scheme breakdowns aligned with official board evaluation rubrics.

100% Free Educational Access

To ensure equitable education for all students appearing for the Maharashtra SSC examinations, BoardsBuddy is providing all study materials completely free of charge for the 2025–2026 academic year. There are no hidden subscription charges or payment paywalls.

To access our custom notes, short revision sheets, chapter-wise question banks, and solutions, all you need is a free student account. Textbooks and past year board papers remain publicly accessible without registration.
