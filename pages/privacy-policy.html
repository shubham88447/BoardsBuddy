Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy outlines how BoardsBuddy collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our educational website.

1. Information We Collect

BoardsBuddy is an educational study portal designed for students. We practice strict data minimization and only collect details necessary for providing authentication and tailored study access:

  • Account Credentials: When you create a student account, we collect your email address, display name, and password. Passwords are cryptographically hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Academic Preferences: Your selected education board (e.g., Maharashtra State Board) and class (Class 10 SSC) to deliver relevant study materials.
  • Technical Session Data: Standard HTTP request logs, browser user agents, and IP addresses automatically processed by our hosting infrastructure (Vercel) to ensure site reliability and prevent automated abuse.

2. Authentication & Supabase Infrastructure

Our authentication service is powered by Supabase, an open-source, industry-standard backend platform. When you register or sign in, authentication is validated through Supabase Auth using secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Your session token is stored locally in your browser's local storage so you remain signed in while moving between study sections without repeated login prompts.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information collected on BoardsBuddy is strictly utilized for:

  • Granting authenticated access to protected educational sections, including Notes, Short Notes, Chapter-wise PYQs, and PYQ Solutions.
  • Remembering your user interface preferences, such as Dark Mode or Light Mode.
  • Maintaining the security of educational materials through database Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Public vs. Protected Content

Visitors can browse and read official Maharashtra State Board Textbooks and full Previous Year Question (PYQ) Papers completely anonymously without creating an account or providing any personal details. Access to teacher notes, quick revision formulas, and chapter-wise answer solutions requires a verified student login.

5. Local Storage and Cookies

BoardsBuddy uses browser localStorage to store your theme choice (boardsbuddy_theme), board selection (boardsbuddy_board), and local session authentication keys. We do not use third-party tracking pixels or behavioral advertising cookies.

6. Data Security & Storage

All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Database records are protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) rules that restrict record modifications strictly to verified administrators.

7. Contacting the Website Operator

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to request deletion of your account and associated profile data, please contact the website maintainer via our official GitHub repository at GitHub Issues or submit an inquiry through our Contact Us page.
